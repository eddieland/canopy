## 39. A tick without the overseer

Once the harness decides what needs no judgment (§37), most ticks leave the overseer nothing: overnight, while PRs wait
on a human, after a run ends with nothing to classify. So the overseer runs only when the journal leaves it something
to judge, and reads the journal alone, and the loop ticks when a poll sees something new rather than when a timer runs
out. A tick that runs no overseer, and what it still checks, is §6 step 5; the polls that end the wait, and idle
pacing's count of the overseer's runs alone, are §6's too, and spend's count of such a tick at nothing §11's. The
harness's `wait` on a stale or unobserved subject is §37's, why config requires a `tracker` source §30's, and the
overseer's definition, which reaches no server but canopy's, §13's. TRUST_MODEL T1, T3, and T6 hold what each lets the
loop do. What is left here is open.
