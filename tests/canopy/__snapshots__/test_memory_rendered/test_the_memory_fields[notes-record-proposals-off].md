- `notes`: At most 5 notes for this ticket's later runs, about this ticket's work that the next run would otherwise have to find again. A note ends with its ticket, so it holds only what is true of this ticket alone: what would hold for another ticket too, such as what the tools or your access here can or cannot do, or how a repository's tests must be run, is not a note. Not what your summary, the branch, or its commits already say, and not what you did: those reach the next run anyway. Leave it out when you have none, since no note is better than one that would not help. Kept by canopy, never posted, and read by the operator: mention none of it on the ticket or a PR.
  - `key`: Lowercase words joined by hyphens, at most 40 characters, starting with the kind of note: `cause-` for a cause of this ticket's problem you found, `dead-end-` for an approach that failed and why, `check-` for how to reproduce or verify it; then its subject, as in `cause-ci-timeout`. A note under a key already written replaces it, so to revise one, keep its key.
  - `text`: Plain text, line breaks sparingly, at most 400 characters, in your own words, in this shape: `<what holds>. Evidence: <a path and line, a test, a command and its outcome, or a commit>. Next: <what a later run should do or skip>.` Never ticket, PR, or log text copied in, never a secret.
